> "You need to store unprocessed data from IoT devices for a new machine learning pipeline. The storage solution should be a centralized and highly available repository.

> What AWS storage service do you choose to store the unprocessed data?

Key words phrases of the question
- `Stored process data`
- `Pipeline`
- `Centralized`
- `highly available`
- `AWS storage service`

EFS is a reasonable choice but S3 is better because of it's direct integration with other services.  Additionally S3 is better equipped with variety of data, structured, semi-structured and unstructured... Though that may also be true for EFS.

> You are designing a highly scalable data repository for your machine learning pipeline.  You need immediate access to the processed data from your pipeline for 6 months.  Your unprocessed data must be accessible within 12 hours for stored for 6 years

> What is your cost-effective storage solution.

Noticed `processed` and `unprocessed`

They go on to change requirements that the storage solution must support SQL Queries.. We wouldn't use dynamoDB or Redshift for some reason.  They say Athena would be the best option for SQL query functionality ( I assume Athena queries object in S3). 

The solution, without SQL, was S3 with life cycle events to move data about.  Because we have processed/unprocessed and different retention requirements for each, we have two life cycle policies.
